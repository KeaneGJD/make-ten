How to Play

🎯 Hit the targetTap a number, choose an operator, then tap the next number. Equations calculate left-to-right.
⭐ CampaignComplete the required number of solutions before your moves run out. Campaign progression unlocks mechanics for every other mode.
📅 Daily ChallengeSolve five puzzles generated from the mechanics you have unlocked. Complete one Daily per calendar day.
⏱️ ArcadeYou have 60 seconds. Correct equations score points and build a combo. Mistakes reset the combo.
♾️ EndlessKeep solving generated puzzles. You have three strikes before the run ends.
